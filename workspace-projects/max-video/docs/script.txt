My dog Max had barked at the same wall at 3 AM every night for a week, and I was about to give him away.

The first night, I figured he heard a mouse. I pressed my ear against the wall and heard nothing. "Max, go to sleep!" He whined and clawed at the paint like his life depended on it.

By Thursday, I hadn't slept in four days. My girlfriend rolled over and groaned, "It's your dog, you fix it." "The neighbor is 84, he's asleep by 7!" I dragged Max out by his collar, but he broke free and sprinted right back to the wall.

That morning, I wrote a post: Free to a good home: one loud dog. I hit save as a draft and went to bed angry.

At 3 AM, Max started again, but this time he wasn't barking. He was howling, throwing his whole body against the wall. I opened my mouth to yell, and then I heard it through the drywall: thump... thump... thump.

My stomach dropped. I ran into the hallway in my socks and pounded on Mr. Harlan's door. "Sir, are you okay?!" Nothing. Then the weak sound of someone tapping back. I called 911, and by the time the paramedics broke the door down, Max was already scratching at the bathroom.

Mr. Harlan was on the floor. He'd fallen hours earlier and couldn't reach his phone. The paramedic looked at me and said, "Another hour, and we wouldn't have been having this conversation."

I went back to my apartment and deleted the post. Max got a whole steak that night.

And Mr. Harlan? Every Sunday now, he knocks on our wall twice, and Max barks back.
