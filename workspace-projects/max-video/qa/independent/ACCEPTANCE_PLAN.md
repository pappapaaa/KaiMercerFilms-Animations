## Preview gates (frames 0–299, 30 fps, 1080×1920)

1. **Pipeline:** actual repo bodyFront/headBlob face library for humans, cardShade and sparse painted world materials, ink → lens pre → lens main → bloom → Neutral tone mapping → grade. New dog is an original compatible asset, not claimed as original repo anatomy. Derived night settings are documented as adaptations.
2. **Night legibility:** 3AM reads visually; warm localized practical against cool dark architecture, with Max's head, muzzle, gaze, legs and wall visible. Colored darks rather than black crush; no day-sky glow. Faces and dog do not bloom. Wides stay sufficiently sharp to establish geography, close-ups selectively soft behind focus.
3. **Anatomy/contact:** Max reads as one coherent dog with consistent muzzle/head/ear attachment, four identifiable legs when framing permits, grounded paws and directional tail. Bark jaw/neck action does not detach body parts or slide planted feet. Human feet, hips and hands contact the intended surfaces/props.
4. **Causality/geography:** Max visibly addresses the shared wall, not owner/camera; angle and gaze remain consistent. Owner notices Max and wall. First ten seconds does not reveal the fallen neighbour prematurely. Clock/wall/corridor relationships remain physically plausible.
5. **Motion/camera:** examine sequential full-size samples across bark anticipation→attack→recovery and shot transition. Distinguish actual performance from static tableau with camera drift. Camera reframes serve clock, dog/wall and owner response; no unmotivated constant wobble.
6. **Caption/audio timing:** exact source narration starts at zero; first words are not clipped, subtitle wraps are clear, keyed-word emphasis does not outrun speech. Cuts/cues use actual narration anchors, not arbitrary average reading speed. Preview duration/frame count and decoded output verified.
7. **Delivery:** native 1080×1920/30, no upscaled lower-resolution stand-in. Full 300 decoded video frames; intact audio. Source snapshot/render manifest traceably identify the reviewed version.
