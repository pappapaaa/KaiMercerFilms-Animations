I called in sick on Friday so I could go to a baseball game. I coughed into the phone as hard as I could. "Boss, I can barely get out of bed." He sighed. "Get some rest. We'll manage." I hung up, grinning like a genius. An hour later, I was in the stands in my lucky jersey, hot dog in hand. In the fifth inning, the big screen lit up with the Fan Cam, and the crowd roared. The camera swept past a kid, past a grandma, and then landed on me: mouth stuffed with hot dog, waving a giant foam finger. Everybody cheered. I waved back like a celebrity. Then I noticed the man sitting right behind me, waving at the camera too. Hawaiian shirt. Giant beer. My boss. We locked eyes. My foam finger slowly dropped. For ten whole seconds, neither of us breathed. Then I ducked behind my hot dog. On Monday morning, I walked into the office with my heart pounding. There was a sticky note on my desk: "You didn't see me. I didn't see you. Feel better soon." At the 9 AM meeting, my boss stood up. "Great news, team. Starting this month, everyone gets one extra personal day." He glanced at me. "Just stay away from the Fan Cam."
